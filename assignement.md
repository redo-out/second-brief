Project context
The company Plomb'Express, specializing in plumbing work, sanitary installation and repairs, wishes to develop its online presence in order to present its services and facilitate contact with its customers. She calls on a digital agency to create a modern showcase website, accessible and adapted to computers and smartphones.

As a junior web developer, you are responsible for continuing the development of the site based on the home page mockups (desktop and mobile) provided by the client. Your mission is to integrate this page while respecting the models, then to design and develop the other pages of the site while maintaining a coherent visual identity.

Features requested
The site must contain at least four pages, accessible from a common navigation bar.

Page 1 — Welcome
The desktop and mobile models of this page are provided and serve as a graphic reference for the entire site.

You must:

Integrate the home page in HTML5 and CSS3 in accordance with the templates provided.
Respect the colors, typographies, spacing and arrangement of elements.
Reproduce both versions: desktop and mobile.
Ensure functional navigation to other pages.
Page 2 — Services
You must design and develop this page while respecting the graphic identity of the home page.

A section presenting the services.
At least six services: troubleshooting, sanitary installation, leak repair, unblocking, maintenance and renovation.
For each service: an image or icon, a title and a description.
A call to action button to the Contact page.
Page 3 — About
A presentation of the company and its history.
A section presenting its values and commitments.
A presentation of your team or areas of expertise.
A section highlighting its main advantages.
Page 4 — Contact
Company contact details: address, telephone and email.
Opening hours.
A form containing the fields: name, email, telephone, requested service and message.
A "Send" button.
**The form is static: no actual processing or sending of data is requested.**

Bonus features
A gallery of the company's achievements.
An FAQ section.
CSS animations and transitions.
A dark mode.
Using Bootstrap or Tailwind CSS to facilitate integration and responsiveness.
Important: using Bootstrap or Tailwind CSS is optional. If you choose one of these frameworks, you must use custom CSS in at least one section of the site. An implementation based solely on the framework classes does not meet this requirement.

User Stories
As a visitor, I want to discover the company from the home page in order to understand its activity.
As a potential customer, I want to consult the services in order to find the service suited to my needs.
As a visitor, I want to know the company's values and commitments in order to assess its professionalism.
As a customer, I want to access the contact details and the form in order to prepare a quote request.
As a mobile user, I want to view the site on my smartphone to easily access information.
Technical constraints
Mandatory technologies: HTML5 and CSS3.

Optional technologies (bonus): Bootstrap or Tailwind CSS.

The following requirements must be met:

Use HTML5 semantic tags.
Use CSS3 for formatting and customization.
Faithfully reproduce the desktop and mobile models provided for the home page.
Design the other pages while respecting the same visual identity.
Apply a Mobile-First approach.
Ensure responsive display on mobile and desktop only.
Use Flexbox and/or CSS Grid as needed.
Respect good accessibility practices: alternative texts, labels, contrasts and keyboard navigation.
Organize HTML, CSS and image files in a clear tree structure.
Use Git and GitHub for versioning.
Perform at least one commit each work day, with a clear message representative of the modifications made.
Do not use a backend or database.
